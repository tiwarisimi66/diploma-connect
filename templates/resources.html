Competitive Exams & Lateral Entry Guides

Govt Recruitment All India Level
Staff Selection Commission — Junior Engineer (SSC JE)

The premier central government exam for 3-year diploma holders in Civil, Mechanical, and Electrical engineering. Posts include CPWD, MES, and Central Water Commission.

Paper 1 (CBT): 200 Marks (50 Reasoning, 50 General Awareness, 100 Technical Domain)
Paper 2 (CBT Technical): 300 Marks in-depth engineering problem-solving
Ask AI for SSC JE Strategy
Railway Recruitment Indian Railways
Railway Recruitment Board — Junior Engineer (RRB JE)

Recruitment for Junior Engineers across railway zones in mechanical workshops, diesel/electric loco sheds, track maintenance, and signaling.

CBT Stage 1: Math (30), Reasoning (25), General Awareness (15), General Science (30)
CBT Stage 2: 100 Marks Technical Abilities + 50 Non-Tech
RRB JE Eligibility Tips

Placement & NATS Apprenticeship Toolkit

National Apprenticeship Training Scheme (NATS)
Govt Stipend

The Government of India's official apprenticeship portal for engineering diploma graduates. It provides a 1-year practical industry training with a mandatory monthly stipend (typically ₹8,000 to ₹12,000/month) backed by the Ministry of Education.

Registration Checklist:
  1. Keep final semester marksheet and Provisional Diploma Certificate ready.
  2. Aadhaar card linked with an active mobile number and bank account.
  3. Register at the official portal: portal.mhrdnats.gov.in.
  4. Search and apply for apprentice drives at PSUs like BHEL, Indian Oil, and private plants.

Recruiter Ready

Diploma recruiters scan resumes in under 10 seconds. Keep your resume strictly to 1 page with the following clean ordering:
